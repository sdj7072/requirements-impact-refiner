## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Should a permanent shared link remain usable after the resource permissions change? | No: remove automatic time expiry and make routine key rotation transparent, while permission changes and explicit revocation still invalidate the link. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003` | Preserves the current authorization boundary and limits stale access, but permanent means durable rather than irrevocable. |
| Should a permanent shared link remain usable after the resource permissions change? | Yes, until explicitly revoked: the link survives time, permission changes, and routine key rotation, but explicit link revocation or resource deletion invalidates it. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004` | Gives recipients a stable capability independent of later ACL edits, but increases stale-access and privacy risk and requires a reliable revocation record. |
