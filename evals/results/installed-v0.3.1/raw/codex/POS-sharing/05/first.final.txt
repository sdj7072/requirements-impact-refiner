## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What lifecycle should permanent shared links have? | Stable revocable link (recommended): no scheduled expiry; survives key rotation and non-security-sensitive permission edits; explicit unshare, resource deletion, or a security-sensitive policy change terminates access. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005` | Provides a durable URL while retaining security controls; likely requires server-side share identity and live policy evaluation. |
| What lifecycle should permanent shared links have? | Non-expiring signed token: honor old verification material while retaining permission-change revocation. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005` | Smaller permission-semantics change, but links are not permanent across permission edits and long-lived key trust is sensitive. |
| What lifecycle should permanent shared links have? | Irrevocable bearer link: once issued, it survives permission changes and key rotation indefinitely. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005` | Strongest permanence, but leaked links and authorization mistakes cannot be reliably corrected. |
