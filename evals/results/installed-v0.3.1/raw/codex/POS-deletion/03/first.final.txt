## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What must “immediately” mean for account deletion? | Immediate logical deletion (recommended): revoke access and hide the account in the request, retain the account and finance records for 30 days, then let the worker hard-delete them. | IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004, IMP-005 | Smallest schema impact and preserves the worker, but the account row and permitted retained data still exist during the retention window. |
| What must “immediately” mean for account deletion? | Immediate anonymized deletion: revoke access and erase or anonymize non-finance personal data in the request, retain only the minimum account tombstone and finance records for 30 days, then let the worker purge them. | IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004, IMP-005 | Stronger immediate privacy outcome, but requires precise data-classification and anonymization design. |
| What must “immediately” mean for account deletion? | Immediate physical account-row deletion: first redesign invoices to reference an independent retained finance subject, then delete the account synchronously while the worker handles remaining retention cleanup. | IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004, IMP-005 | Matches literal physical deletion, but requires a schema migration and substantially increases integrity, rollout, and rollback risk. |
