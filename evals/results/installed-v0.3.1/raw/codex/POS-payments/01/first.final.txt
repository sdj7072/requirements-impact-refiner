## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Which outcomes should the system automatically retry? | **Recommended:** Retry only provider-confirmed retryable failures. Keep timeouts and unknown results pending, reconcile through webhook or provider lookup, and retry only after non-capture is confirmed using the original logical-charge idempotency key. | IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004, IMP-005 | Safest against duplicate capture and premature failure rendering, but recovery may be slower and requires authoritative reconciliation. |
| Which outcomes should the system automatically retry? | Retry failures and timeouts immediately with the same idempotency key and bounded backoff. | IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004, IMP-005 | Faster recovery, but safety depends on unverified provider idempotency scope and retention; status remains ambiguous until settlement. |
| Which outcomes should the system automatically retry? | Retry failures and timeouts as new charge attempts with new idempotency keys and bounded backoff. | IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004, IMP-005 | Broadest interpretation, but timeout-after-capture can produce duplicate charges and conflicting status. |
