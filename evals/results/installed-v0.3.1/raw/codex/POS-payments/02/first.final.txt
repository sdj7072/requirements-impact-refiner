## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Which retry contract should define a “failed charge”? | A — Outcome-aware (recommended): retry only confirmed transient no-capture failures as a new attempt; keep timeouts or other ambiguous outcomes pending for webhook/provider reconciliation; do not retry terminal declines. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005` | Lowest duplicate-capture and customer-harm risk, but some charges wait for reconciliation and not every non-success response is retried immediately. |
| Which retry contract should define a “failed charge”? | B — Same-key transport replay: retry transient failures and ambiguous timeouts with the exact same idempotency key, under a bounded policy, while status remains pending; do not retry terminal declines. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005` | Faster recovery, but safe use depends on verified provider guarantees for same-key replay, parameter matching, and key retention. |
| Which retry contract should define a “failed charge”? | C — Literal all-failures retry: retry every non-success result, including terminal declines and timeouts, as a fresh attempt with a fresh idempotency key. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005`, `IMP-006` | Closest to the literal request, but can duplicate post-timeout captures, repeat terminal declines, increase fees, and create unbounded customer and operational risk unless separately constrained. |
