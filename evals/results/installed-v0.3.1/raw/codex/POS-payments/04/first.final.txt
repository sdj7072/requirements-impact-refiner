## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Which outcomes should enter automatic charge retry? | Reconcile ambiguous outcomes (Recommended): retry only definitive retryable failures with bounded attempts and backoff; hold timeouts or unknown outcomes for webhook or provider-status reconciliation before any new charge attempt. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005` | Strongest protection against duplicate capture and retry storms, but recovery from ambiguous timeouts waits for settlement evidence and requires a reconciliation path. |
| Which outcomes should enter automatic charge retry? | Replay every non-success outcome with the same idempotency key, including timeouts. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005` | Faster and mechanically simple, but safety remains blocked until provider key scope, retention, and replay semantics are verified. |
| Which outcomes should enter automatic charge retry? | Start a new charge attempt for every non-success outcome, including timeouts. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005` | Maximizes retry coverage but exposes customers to duplicate capture when a timeout follows capture and can amplify persistent failures. |
