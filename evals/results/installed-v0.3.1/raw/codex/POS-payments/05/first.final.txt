## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Which retry policy should govern definitive failures and ambiguous timeout-after-capture outcomes? | **Reconcile-first, failure-classified retry (recommended):** retry definitive transient failures automatically; treat timeout-after-capture as unknown and reconcile provider state or webhook settlement before retrying, retaining the same logical payment identity. | IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004 | Lowest duplicate-charge risk and clearest status semantics, but recovery may wait for reconciliation and requires provider-status support. |
| Which retry policy should govern definitive failures and ambiguous timeout-after-capture outcomes? | **Strict literal retry:** retry every non-success response, including timeout-after-capture, using the original idempotency key. | IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004 | Closest to the literal request, but safe only if the provider’s deduplication scope and lifetime are verified; otherwise duplicate capture remains critical. |
