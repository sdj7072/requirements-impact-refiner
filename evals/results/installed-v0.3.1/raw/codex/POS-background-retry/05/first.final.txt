## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What should signal operators after attempt five while the export remains retryable forever? | Emit `dead_letter` once at attempt five as a nonterminal alert signal, then continue retries. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003` | Preserves current alert wiring, but changes the event’s terminal meaning and may break unknown consumers. |
| What should signal operators after attempt five while the export remains retryable forever? | Emit a new nonterminal prolonged-failure event once at attempt five and migrate alerts to it. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003` | Keeps `dead_letter` semantically terminal, but requires an event-contract and alert-consumer migration. |
| What should signal operators after attempt five while the export remains retryable forever? | Emit no attempt-threshold event and accept loss of the current dead-letter-driven alert path. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002` | Minimizes worker event changes, but removes the supplied failure alert unless separate monitoring exists. |
