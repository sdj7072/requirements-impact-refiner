## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What should replace the terminal dead-letter signal while an export continues retrying? | Emit a new nonterminal stalled event at attempt five and a recovery event after eventual success; update the alert consumer. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002` | Keeps semantics truthful and gives alerts a clean lifecycle, but changes producer and consumer contracts. |
| What should replace the terminal dead-letter signal while an export continues retrying? | Continue emitting `dead_letter` at attempt five while leaving the job retryable. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002` | Minimizes consumer changes, but redefines a conventionally terminal event and may mislead other consumers. |
| What should replace the terminal dead-letter signal while an export continues retrying? | Replace the event dependency with a failure-threshold metric that clears after success. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002` | Avoids a new event contract, but moves correlation into monitoring and may reduce per-job detail. |
