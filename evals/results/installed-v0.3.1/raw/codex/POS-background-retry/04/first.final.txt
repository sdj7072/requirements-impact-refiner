## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What event and alert behavior should occur when a still-retrying export reaches its fifth consecutive failed attempt? | Keep emitting `dead_letter` once at the fifth failure while leaving the job retryable forever. | `IMP-003`, `IMP-005` | Preserves the current alert consumer, but `dead_letter` no longer means terminal. |
| What event and alert behavior should occur when a still-retrying export reaches its fifth consecutive failed attempt? | **Recommended:** emit a new nonterminal `export_retry_threshold` event once, migrate alerts to it, and reserve `dead_letter` for terminal cases. | `IMP-003`, `IMP-005` | Preserves clear semantics and actionable alerts, but requires an event-contract and alert-consumer migration. |
| What event and alert behavior should occur when a still-retrying export reaches its fifth consecutive failed attempt? | Emit no threshold event and replace per-job dead-letter alerts with retry-health metrics or aggregate alerts. | `IMP-003`, `IMP-005` | Simplifies the job event path, but changes alert granularity and may lose immediate per-job visibility. |
