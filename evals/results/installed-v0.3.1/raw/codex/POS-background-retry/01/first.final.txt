## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Which operational contract should define “retry forever” and persistent-failure alerting? | A — Durable unbounded retries with capped exponential backoff and jitter; emit a new non-terminal persistent-failure event at attempt five and periodically thereafter; reserve dead_letter for truly terminal failures. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005`, `IMP-006` | Recommended: preserves retryability and alert visibility with explicit event semantics, but requires a new event contract and alert-consumer migration. |
| Which operational contract should define “retry forever” and persistent-failure alerting? | B — Durable unbounded retries using the existing retry delay; continue emitting dead_letter at attempt five as a warning while retries continue. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005`, `IMP-006` | Minimizes alert wiring changes, but makes dead_letter non-terminal, risks consumer incompatibility, and may create uncontrolled retry load. |
| Which operational contract should define “retry forever” and persistent-failure alerting? | C — Durable unbounded retries with capped exponential backoff and jitter; stop emitting per-job failure events and alert only from job-age or retry-count metrics. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-004`, `IMP-005`, `IMP-006` | Keeps event semantics clean and can reduce alert noise, but requires reliable metrics and changes alerting from an event contract to an operational threshold. |
