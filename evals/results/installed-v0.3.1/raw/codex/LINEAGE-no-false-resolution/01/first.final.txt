## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What delivery guarantee should apply when a webhook attempt times out after the provider may already have processed it? | Keep retrying and require a stable idempotency mechanism across attempts. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003` | Preserves retry-based reliability and reduces duplicate effects, but changes the sender-receiver contract and requires receiver compatibility and verification. |
| What delivery guarantee should apply when a webhook attempt times out after the provider may already have processed it? | Do not automatically retry ambiguous provider timeouts. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002` | Avoids timeout-driven duplicate attempts, but can lose deliveries when the provider did not process the timed-out attempt. |
| What delivery guarantee should apply when a webhook attempt times out after the provider may already have processed it? | Keep the current retry behavior and explicitly accept possible duplicate delivery. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003` | Avoids a compatibility change, but leaves recipients responsible for duplicate effects and requires a clearly documented at-least-once contract. |
