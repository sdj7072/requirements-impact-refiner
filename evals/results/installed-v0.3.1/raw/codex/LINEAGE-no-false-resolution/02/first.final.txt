## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Which delivery guarantee should govern retries after ambiguous provider timeouts? | At-least-once with a stable idempotency key: continue timeout retries, reuse one identifier for the logical delivery, and define the recipient deduplication contract. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004` | Preserves recovery from failed attempts and can suppress duplicate effects, but requires a versioned interface contract, key-lifecycle rules, recipient support, and operational verification. |
| Which delivery guarantee should govern retries after ambiguous provider timeouts? | Retry only when safe replay is confirmed: retry ambiguous timeouts automatically only for providers with established idempotency support; otherwise surface the delivery for alerting or reconciliation. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004` | Reduces unprotected duplicates, but can delay or miss delivery without an operational recovery path and requires provider-specific capability evidence. |
| Which delivery guarantee should govern retries after ambiguous provider timeouts? | At-least-once with duplicate risk explicitly accepted: keep timeout retries, document possible duplicates, and make recipients responsible for deduplication without a guaranteed protocol mechanism. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003`, `IMP-004` | Minimizes sender-side change and preserves retry availability, but retains the critical duplicate-side-effect risk and relies on undocumented recipient behavior. |
