## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Which delivery guarantee should govern an ambiguous provider timeout? | Preserve automatic retries and define a stable idempotency key plus a documented consumer deduplication contract. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003` | Preserves at-least-once recovery; repeated requests remain possible, and safe effects depend on consumer compliance and key-retention semantics. |
| Which delivery guarantee should govern an ambiguous provider timeout? | Add a coordinated acknowledgement and reconciliation contract before retrying an ambiguous delivery. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003` | Can distinguish more outcomes, but adds sender state, protocol complexity, provider integration requirements, and recovery logic. |
| Which delivery guarantee should govern an ambiguous provider timeout? | Do not automatically retry ambiguous timeouts; record the outcome as unknown and require explicit reconciliation. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002`, `IMP-003` | Reduces automatic duplicate delivery, but increases missed-delivery risk and operational burden. |
