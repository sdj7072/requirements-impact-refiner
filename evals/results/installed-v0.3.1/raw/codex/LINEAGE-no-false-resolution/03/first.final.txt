## Decision Needed

| Question | Option | Impact IDs | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What delivery guarantee should govern retries after an ambiguous provider timeout? | At-least-once delivery with a stable, documented idempotency key reused across every attempt for the same webhook event. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002` | Preserves retries while enabling deduplication, but requires a key contract, persistence lifetime, and provider cooperation or an internal deduplication boundary. |
| What delivery guarantee should govern retries after an ambiguous provider timeout? | At-most-once delivery: do not retry when a timeout leaves the provider outcome unknown. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002` | Avoids retry-caused duplicates after ambiguous timeouts, but can permanently lose deliveries whose first attempt was not processed. |
| What delivery guarantee should govern retries after an ambiguous provider timeout? | Retain timeout retries without an idempotency guarantee and document the webhook contract as potentially duplicative. | `IMP-001`, `IMP-002` | Minimizes behavioral change, but knowingly retains duplicate-delivery exposure and shifts deduplication responsibility to consumers. |
