## Decision Needed

- Question: What duplicate-delivery contract should retry_delivery enforce after an ambiguous provider timeout?

### Option 1

- Option: Require a stable event idempotency key and suppress or coalesce duplicate attempts.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Prevents duplicate side effects but requires a durable deduplication record, atomic concurrency semantics, and a defined retention window.

### Option 2

- Option: Keep at-least-once delivery and explicitly allow duplicates.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Keeps retry handling simpler, but pushes idempotency onto every webhook consumer and leaves duplicate side effects as an accepted operational risk.

### Option 3

- Option: Do not automatically retry ambiguous provider timeouts.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Avoids timeout-driven duplicates, but may lose deliveries when the provider did not accept the first attempt and requires reconciliation or manual recovery.
