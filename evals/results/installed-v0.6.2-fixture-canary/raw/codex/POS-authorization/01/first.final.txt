## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: authorization.workspace_edit role policy
- Possible issue: A broad authorization change could accidentally admit unintended roles or remove existing owner/admin access.
- Affected feature or user: All workspace members and every project protected by authorize_project_edit.
- Trigger: A user attempts to edit a project with a member, owner, admin, or unsupported role.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Limit the policy change to adding member to the explicit permitted-role tuple and verify positive and negative role cases.
- Status: accepted

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Edits performed by newly authorized members
- Possible issue: Member edits could become less attributable if the existing role-aware audit contract were changed or bypassed.
- Affected feature or user: Operators and reviewers relying on project.edit.audited records.
- Trigger: A newly authorized member edits a project and audit data is emitted.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Leave events/audit.py unchanged and verify emit_actor_role_audit continues to return event, actor, and role.
- Status: accepted
