- Changed feature: Remove profile.displayName decoding beginning at the mobile profile decoder while accounting for the desktop serializer.

- Trigger: Removal or expiry of profile.displayName while either detected client path still reads, writes, or stores the field.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Preserve the field for one release and verify or retire both the mobile decoder and desktop serializer before that release window expires.
- Status: mitigated
