## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Webhook delivery retry behavior in retry_delivery
- Possible issue: A retry after an ambiguous provider timeout may deliver the same event twice and repeat downstream side effects.
- Affected feature or user: Webhook recipients and any downstream state changes triggered by the event
- Trigger: The provider accepts or processes the first attempt but its response times out, after which retry_delivery sends another attempt.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Obtain an explicit decision selecting deduplication, reconciliation, or accepted at-least-once delivery; a tentative stakeholder opinion is insufficient.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: The result and observable contract of retry_delivery
- Possible issue: Callers may observe a changed return value or delivery outcome without a defined compatibility contract.
- Affected feature or user: Callers of retry_delivery and operational handling of failed webhook attempts
- Trigger: Retry logic starts returning a status, wrapping the event, suppressing a retry, or raising an error instead of returning the event unchanged.
- Severity: medium
- Prevention or check: Document and approve the timeout and return-value contract, preserve event passthrough unless intentionally changed, and add contract tests before implementation.
- Status: blocked

## Decision Needed

- Question: Which duplicate-delivery policy should govern retry_delivery when a provider timeout leaves acceptance of the original attempt unknown?

### Option 1

- Option: Require a stable idempotency key and durable deduplication before automatic retry.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002
- Trade-off: Best duplicate suppression, but requires a key contract, storage/retention rules, and defined behavior when the deduplication store is unavailable.

### Option 2

- Option: Do not automatically retry ambiguous provider timeouts; surface the delivery for reconciliation.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002
- Trade-off: Avoids retry-created duplicates, but may leave accepted-unknown or genuinely failed deliveries pending and requires an operational reconciliation path.

### Option 3

- Option: Retain automatic retry and explicitly accept at-least-once delivery.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002
- Trade-off: Keeps availability and simple retry behavior, but requires an explicit approved acceptance and downstream consumers that tolerate duplicates.
