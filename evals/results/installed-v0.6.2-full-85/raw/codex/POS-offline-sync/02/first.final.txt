## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Reconnect synchronization of offline.edits
- Possible issue: Queued edits could bypass model normalization and timestamp stamping.
- Affected feature or user: Offline-created records and edits replayed after reconnect.
- Trigger: The app reconnects with one or more pending queue items.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Drain the queue only through models/record.py and verify every replayed mutation uses that path.
- Status: mitigated

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Ordered reconnect replay
- Possible issue: Continuing after a failed edit or acknowledging too early can reorder dependent edits or lose local changes.
- Affected feature or user: The failed queue item and all later locally created edits.
- Trigger: A record mutation fails partway through reconnect synchronization.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Process sequentially, acknowledge after success, and stop while retaining the failed suffix.
- Status: detected

### Impact IMP-003

- Changed feature: Reconnect reconciliation against server deletions
- Possible issue: An offline edit can resurrect a server-deleted record, especially after its tombstone expires.
- Affected feature or user: Records deleted while a client remains offline.
- Trigger: A queued edit targets a record with a live or already-expired deletion tombstone.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Reject live-tombstone conflicts and surface expired-window conflicts instead of silently recreating records.
- Status: detected

### Impact IMP-004

- Changed feature: Timestamping of replayed offline edits
- Possible issue: Using a client-supplied or queue-time timestamp can violate model ownership and alter conflict resolution.
- Affected feature or user: Consumers that compare updated_at values.
- Trigger: A queued edit is replayed after reconnect.
- Severity: medium
- Prevention or check: Have models/record.py stamp updated_at when the replay mutation is applied and test that client timestamps do not override it.
- Status: detected

## Original Requirement

- ID: REQ-001
- Original request: Sync offline.edits through models/record.py when the app reconnects.
- Source: User request and supplied repository evidence.

## Current Refined Requirement

- ID: REQ-001
- Revision: When the app reconnects, synchronize the offline.edits queue by replaying each pending edit through the canonical models/record.py mutation path, in the queue's preserved local creation order. models/record.py remains the owner of updated_at stamping. A queue item is removed only after its record mutation succeeds; on failure, retain that item and all later items for retry. Reconnect reconciliation must honor any still-live server tombstone and must not silently recreate a record merely because its 24-hour tombstone has expired.
- Refined by decision: DEC-001
- Supersedes: none
