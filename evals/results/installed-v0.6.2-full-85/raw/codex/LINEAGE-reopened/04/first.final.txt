## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Remove mobile decoding of profile.displayName beginning at decodeDisplayName.
- Possible issue: Older released clients could fail to obtain the display name if the producer stopped supplying the field before those clients aged out.
- Affected feature or user: Released mobile clients that still call decodeDisplayName for profile.displayName.
- Trigger: Deploying the profile field removal without a compatibility overlap.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Retain profile.displayName for the configured one-release migration window while the new mobile release removes its decoder dependency.
- Status: accepted
