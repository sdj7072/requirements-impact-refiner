## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: sharing.link token lifetime
- Possible issue: A remaining expiry value or generated expiry claim would continue to reject links after seven days.
- Affected feature or user: Existing and newly issued sharing links whose validation reads share/tokens.py.
- Trigger: Validation occurs more than seven days after token issuance.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Represent the lifetime as explicitly non-expiring and test validation beyond the old boundary.
- Status: refining

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Permanent sharing.link validity
- Possible issue: Removing time expiry could accidentally bypass the remaining authorization revocation boundary.
- Affected feature or user: Owners and recipients of links after sharing permissions are reduced or removed.
- Trigger: A permission change occurs after a token has been issued.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Keep REVOKE_ON_PERMISSION_CHANGE enabled and verify that an otherwise non-expiring token is rejected after a permission change.
- Status: refining

### Impact IMP-003

- Changed feature: Permanent sharing.link validity across signing-key rotation
- Possible issue: Tokens may become unverifiable at rotation, making “permanent” mean at most 90 days, or retaining keys indefinitely may enlarge the compromise window.
- Affected feature or user: Every sharing.link token issued before a signing-key rotation.
- Trigger: The active signing key rotates or a retired key is removed.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Choose and document rotation semantics, then test tokens issued before and validated after rotation.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-004

- Changed feature: sharing.link token lifetime
- Possible issue: A leaked token remains usable indefinitely while permissions remain unchanged.
- Affected feature or user: Resources accessible through copied, logged, forwarded, or otherwise exposed sharing links.
- Trigger: A token is disclosed and the owner does not change the corresponding permission.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Preserve explicit revocation and decide whether an additional manual revocation mechanism or documented risk acceptance is required.
- Status: refining

## Decision Needed

- Question: What should “permanent” mean when signing keys rotate every 90 days?

### Option 1

- Option: Valid until explicitly revoked, including across routine key rotations.
- Affected impacts: IMP-003, IMP-004
- Trade-off: Matches the ordinary meaning of permanent, but requires retaining old verification capability or adopting a rotation-independent token design; compromised credentials need explicit revocation.

### Option 2

- Option: No EXPIRY_DAYS limit, but allow key rotation to invalidate older tokens.
- Affected impacts: IMP-003
- Trade-off: Smallest change in share/tokens.py, but tokens are not actually permanent and may stop working after rotation.

### Option 3

- Option: Keep the stable link permanent while transparently replacing its signed credential.
- Affected impacts: IMP-003, IMP-004
- Trade-off: Preserves the user-visible URL across rotations without retaining old signing keys indefinitely, but requires indirection or reissuance beyond the current files.
