## Decision Needed

What should “permanent” mean for `sharing.link` tokens?

1. **No time expiry**, but keep permission-change revocation and existing signing-key rotation semantics. This is the safest interpretation.
2. **No time expiry and survive key rotation**, but keep permission-change revocation.
3. **Irrevocable indefinitely**, including across permission changes and key rotations. This carries critical authorization and key-compromise risks.

The repository does not define a non-expiring sentinel or consumers that clarify whether `EXPIRY_DAYS` should become `None`, `0`, or another value. No files were changed.

Please choose option 1, 2, or 3 and specify the intended no-expiry value if it is defined outside this repository.
