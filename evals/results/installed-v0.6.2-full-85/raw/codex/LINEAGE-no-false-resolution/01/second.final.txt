## Decision Needed

- Question: Which duplicate-delivery control must govern retry_delivery after an ambiguous provider timeout?

### Option 1

- Option: Reuse a stable idempotency key for every attempt of one logical webhook event and require provider-side deduplication.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Strong retry simplicity and duplicate protection, but depends on a documented provider idempotency contract and key-retention window.

### Option 2

- Option: Reconcile the first attempt with the provider before retrying, and retry only when non-delivery is confirmed.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Avoids relying solely on deduplication, but adds status-query latency, provider integration complexity, and handling for inconclusive reconciliation.

### Option 3

- Option: Explicitly approve at-least-once retry semantics and require downstream consumers to deduplicate by a stable event identifier.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Preserves availability during ambiguous failures, but requires an accountable risk-acceptance decision and transfers correctness responsibility to every consumer.
