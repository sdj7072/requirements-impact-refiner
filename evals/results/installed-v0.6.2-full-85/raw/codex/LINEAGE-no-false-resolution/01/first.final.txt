## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Webhook retry behavior after provider timeout
- Possible issue: A retry may deliver the same logical webhook event twice when the first attempt succeeded remotely but its acknowledgement was lost.
- Affected feature or user: Webhook recipients, downstream side effects, delivery accounting, and operators reconciling webhook outcomes
- Trigger: retry_delivery is invoked after an ambiguous provider timeout without a proven idempotency key or provider delivery-status check
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Select and verify an idempotency or reconciliation strategy before changing retry mechanics; retain one stable logical delivery identity across attempts and test the ambiguous-timeout case.
- Status: blocked

## Decision Needed

- Question: Which duplicate-delivery control must govern retry_delivery after an ambiguous provider timeout?

### Option 1

- Option: Reuse a stable idempotency key for every attempt of one logical webhook event and require provider-side deduplication.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Strong retry simplicity and duplicate protection, but depends on a documented provider idempotency contract and key-retention window.

### Option 2

- Option: Reconcile the first attempt with the provider before retrying, and retry only when non-delivery is confirmed.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Avoids relying solely on deduplication, but adds status-query latency, provider integration complexity, and handling for inconclusive reconciliation.

### Option 3

- Option: Keep at-least-once retry semantics and require downstream consumers to deduplicate by a stable event identifier.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Preserves availability during ambiguous failures, but transfers correctness responsibility to every consumer and leaves duplicates possible for non-compliant consumers.
