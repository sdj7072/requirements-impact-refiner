## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Webhook delivery retry behavior after a provider timeout
- Possible issue: The same logical webhook can be delivered more than once when the provider accepted the first attempt but its response timed out.
- Affected feature or user: Webhook recipients, their downstream side effects, and operators reconciling delivery incidents
- Trigger: An ambiguous provider timeout followed by retry_delivery for the same event
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Select and document the delivery guarantee, then verify stable idempotency across attempts or suppress the ambiguous retry; otherwise explicitly accept at-least-once duplicate risk.
- Status: blocked

## Decision Needed

- Question: Which delivery guarantee should retry_delivery enforce after an ambiguous provider timeout?

### Option 1

- Option: Retry with a stable provider-supported idempotency key for the logical event.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Preserves retries and can suppress duplicates, but depends on provider idempotency support and stable key propagation.

### Option 2

- Option: Do not automatically retry an ambiguous timeout.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Avoids retry-created duplicates but can lose deliveries that the provider did not accept.

### Option 3

- Option: Keep at-least-once retries and explicitly accept possible duplicates.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Maximizes delivery attempts with the least implementation work, but recipients must tolerate and reconcile duplicate side effects.
