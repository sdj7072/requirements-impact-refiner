## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Webhook delivery retry behavior in retry_delivery
- Possible issue: The same webhook may be delivered more than once when the first provider request succeeds but its response times out.
- Affected feature or user: Webhook recipients and any downstream side effects triggered by the provider
- Trigger: A provider timeout followed by retry_delivery retrying the event without an effective stable idempotency policy
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Select and document an idempotency strategy, carry its stable identity across attempts, and verify the ambiguous-timeout case before implementation proceeds.
- Status: blocked

## Decision Needed

- Question: Which delivery guarantee should retry_delivery enforce for an ambiguous provider timeout?

### Option 1

- Option: Persist and reuse a stable delivery idempotency key across every retry.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Provides the strongest duplicate protection, but requires durable key/state handling and provider or local deduplication support.

### Option 2

- Option: Use the provider's idempotency-key contract and reuse the same key across retries.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Keeps local state simpler, but safety depends on the provider's documented key scope, retention period, and retry semantics.

### Option 3

- Option: Retain at-least-once delivery and explicitly accept possible duplicates.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001
- Trade-off: Minimizes implementation work, but downstream consumers must tolerate duplicate webhook effects and the high risk remains accepted.
