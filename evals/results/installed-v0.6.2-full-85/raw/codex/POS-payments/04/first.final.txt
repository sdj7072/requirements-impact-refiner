## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Automatic retry of payment.charge failures
- Possible issue: A post-capture timeout may be retried as another charge and debit the customer twice.
- Affected feature or user: Customers, payment records, refunds, and reconciliation
- Trigger: The gateway captures successfully, returns a timeout, and automatic retry runs without proven idempotent replay semantics.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Choose a retry policy that reuses one stable idempotency key for the same logical charge and treats post-capture timeouts as pending until replay/query/webhook reconciliation is safe.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Automatic retry and its visible payment status
- Possible issue: The UI may show failure, retry, or success as final while the original attempt has already captured and settlement remains unresolved.
- Affected feature or user: Customers and support staff reading payment status
- Trigger: Status is rendered after an attempt-level error but before webhook settlement or reconciliation.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Represent ambiguous/retrying charges as pending and transition to a final state only from authoritative settlement or a proven terminal outcome.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-003

- Changed feature: Retry every failed payment.charge automatically
- Possible issue: Permanent failures or prolonged outages may cause retry storms, repeated provider calls, and charges that never reach a terminal operational state.
- Affected feature or user: Gateway capacity, workers, observability, and customer support
- Trigger: A terminal or persistent error is classified as retryable without an attempt cap and backoff.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Define retryable versus terminal errors, a finite attempt limit, backoff, and an exhausted state with observable reason.
- Status: blocked

## Decision Needed

- Question: How should automatic retries handle failures that may occur after capture?

### Option 1

- Option: Retry only with the same idempotency key; keep ambiguous post-capture timeouts pending until safe replay or settlement reconciliation.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003
- Trade-off: Safest default and supports automatic recovery, but requires stable retry state, bounded policy, and pending/reconciliation behavior.

### Option 2

- Option: Retry only failures proven to occur before capture; do not automatically retry ambiguous post-capture timeouts.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003
- Trade-off: Minimizes duplicate-charge risk and implementation scope, but some recoverable ambiguous failures require webhook settlement or manual recovery.

### Option 3

- Option: Retry every reported failure immediately, including post-capture timeouts.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003
- Trade-off: Maximizes retry coverage but is unsafe unless the gateway's idempotent replay and settlement contracts are proven; those contracts are absent here.
