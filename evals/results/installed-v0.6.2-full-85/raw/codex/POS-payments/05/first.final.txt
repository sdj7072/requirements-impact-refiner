# Requirements Impact Report

## Report State

- ID: RPT-001
- Revision: 1
- Phase: pre-decision

## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Automatic retry of failed `payment.charge` attempts
- Possible issue: An ambiguous timeout may be retried as a new capture and charge the customer twice.
- Affected: Customers, payment records, refunds, and reconciliation
- Trigger: The gateway captures funds and then times out before returning the result.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention: Reuse the same idempotency key across attempts and require provider-side deduplication or reconciliation.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Automatic retry of every failed `payment.charge`
- Possible issue: Permanent declines may be retried unnecessarily, while unbounded retries create runaway work and unclear terminal behavior.
- Affected: Gateway traffic, payment latency, customers, and monitoring
- Severity: high
- Prevention: Define retryable failures, a finite attempt limit, backoff, and an exhausted state.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-003

- Changed feature: Charge status during retries
- Possible issue: The UI may display failure although capture succeeded, or success before settlement.
- Affected: Customer-visible payment status
- Trigger: Status renders between an attempt result and authoritative webhook settlement.
- Severity: high
- Prevention: Keep retrying and ambiguous outcomes non-terminal; webhook settlement determines final status.
- Status: blocked

## Current Behavior

- `payments/charge.py` accepts and returns `idempotency_key`, but has no provider call, retry implementation, or deduplication.
- `providers/gateway.py` declares `TIMEOUT_AFTER_CAPTURE = True`; timeout therefore does not prove failure.
- `ui/status.py` renders before webhook settlement.
- No tests, persistence, queue, reconciliation, or webhook implementation exist in the repository.

## Decision Needed

Which retry-safety policy should `payment.charge` use?

1. **Bounded safe retries (recommended):** Retry transient failures with the original idempotency key, keep post-capture timeouts pending for reconciliation, and use a finite attempt limit and backoff.

2. **Pre-capture retries only:** Retry confirmed pre-capture transient failures, but never automatically retry ambiguous timeouts or permanent declines.

3. **Retry every error:** Retry all errors—including post-capture timeouts—with the same idempotency key. This requires guaranteed provider-side deduplication and still needs a finite stopping rule.

Also specify the attempt limit and backoff—for example, **3 total attempts with exponential backoff**.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every attempt for one logical charge uses the original `idempotency_key`.
- A capture-then-timeout scenario cannot produce a second capture.
- Retry eligibility, attempt limit, backoff, and exhaustion behavior are deterministic.
- Before settlement, retrying and ambiguous outcomes render as pending/processing.
- Tests cover transient recovery, permanent failure, exhaustion, capture-then-timeout, and pre-settlement UI state.

## Planning Handoff

Implementation is blocked pending the retry-policy choice, attempt budget/backoff, and provider idempotency or reconciliation contract.
