## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Automatic retries for failed payment.charge attempts
- Possible issue: A timeout after a successful capture may be retried as a new capture and debit the payer twice.
- Affected feature or user: Payers, merchant balances, charge records, and reconciliation
- Trigger: The provider captures funds and the caller observes TIMEOUT_AFTER_CAPTURE as a failure.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Reuse one request-bound idempotency key backed by durable provider/application deduplication, and reconcile ambiguous outcomes before another capture.
- Status: refining

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Retry-driven charge state transitions
- Possible issue: The UI may show failed or retrying even though capture succeeded, or show success before settlement.
- Affected feature or user: Customers and support staff relying on payment status
- Trigger: A charge is retried or has an ambiguous outcome before its settlement webhook.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Expose an explicit pending/unknown state and allow webhook or reconciliation to make the terminal transition.
- Status: refining

### Impact IMP-003

- Changed feature: Retry every failed payment.charge automatically
- Possible issue: Permanent declines or persistent outages can create an endless retry loop and sustained provider load.
- Affected feature or user: Payment workers, provider quotas, operations, and customers awaiting a final state
- Trigger: A non-retryable failure or repeated transient failure is classified only as failed.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Define retryable versus terminal outcomes, bounded attempts with backoff, and a final operational state.
- Status: refining

## Decision Needed

- Question: Which retry contract should govern failures and ambiguous timeout-after-capture outcomes?

### Option 1

- Option: Retry only bounded, definitively transient pre-capture failures with the same request-bound key; mark post-capture timeouts pending and reconcile before any further capture.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003
- Trade-off: Safest against duplicate charges, but not every observed failure immediately produces another capture attempt.

### Option 2

- Option: Retry every non-terminal and ambiguous outcome with the same key, after implementing durable idempotency/deduplication and bounded backoff.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003
- Trade-off: Closest safe interpretation of automatic retry, but requires gateway/storage contracts absent from this repository.

### Option 3

- Option: Retry every observed failure literally with the current key passthrough only.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003
- Trade-off: Smallest local change, but duplicate captures and unbounded retries cannot be prevented with current evidence.
