## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Automatic retry of payment.charge failures
- Possible issue: An attempt that captured funds but timed out can be submitted again and charge the customer twice.
- Affected feature or user: Customers, payment records, refunds, and provider reconciliation
- Trigger: A transport timeout or exception occurs after the gateway captured the payment.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Reuse the exact idempotency key and treat post-capture timeout as pending reconciliation; retry only under a selected provider-safe policy.
- Status: refining

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Retry every failed payment.charge automatically
- Possible issue: Permanent declines, invalid requests, authentication failures, or unbounded repeated failures may be retried even though they cannot succeed.
- Affected feature or user: Gateway load, customer experience, rate limits, and operational stability
- Trigger: Any result is classified generically as failed.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Select and encode a retry policy that distinguishes retryable pre-capture failures, ambiguous outcomes, and terminal failures, with bounded attempts and backoff.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-003

- Changed feature: Automatic background charge retry and reconciliation
- Possible issue: The UI can show failed while capture already succeeded, or show a retry result that is later contradicted by settlement.
- Affected feature or user: Payment status UI and any consumer acting on displayed status
- Trigger: Status is rendered after a request error but before the settlement webhook.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Represent retrying or unknown outcomes as non-final and make authoritative settlement idempotently supersede provisional status.
- Status: refining

### Impact IMP-004

- Changed feature: Operational automatic retries
- Possible issue: A local loop in payments/charge.py would not establish durable retries, provider idempotency, crash recovery, or settlement coordination.
- Affected feature or user: Charge execution, monitoring, restart behavior, and incident response
- Trigger: The process exits, the provider contract differs from assumptions, or settlement arrives while a retry is scheduled.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Define the gateway result/error interface and observable retry lifecycle, then add deterministic tests for retries, reconciliation, and status ordering.
- Status: blocked

## Decision Needed

- Question: What should count as an automatically retryable payment.charge failure, especially when the gateway times out after capture?

### Option 1

- Option: Reconcile first, then retry only retryable failures (recommended)
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004
- Trade-off: Safest: keep the same idempotency key, mark post-capture timeouts pending, reconcile by provider result/webhook, retry only confirmed-not-captured transient failures with bounded attempts and backoff; requires a reconciliation/status contract.

### Option 2

- Option: Immediately retry transient errors with the same idempotency key
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004
- Trade-off: Faster recovery and simpler flow, but safety depends on an explicit provider guarantee that the idempotency key is honored across timeout-after-capture retries; that guarantee is not present in the repository.

### Option 3

- Option: Literally retry every non-success result
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004
- Trade-off: Matches the broad wording, but can retry declines and ambiguous captures, cause retry storms, and risk duplicate charges; unsuitable without additional hard safety constraints.
