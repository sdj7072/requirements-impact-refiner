# Requirements Impact Report

## Report State

- ID: RPT-001
- Revision: 1
- Phase: pre-decision

## Change Impact Summary

### IMP-001 — Critical

Immediate deletion will fail while invoices reference the account. Changing the constraint without defining the invoice lifecycle could erase retained finance records.

### IMP-002 — High

Immediate deletion conflicts with the configured 30-day finance-retention period. Records might be removed prematurely or retain personal account linkage after deletion.

### IMP-003 — High

Queued or retried `account-cleanup` events may operate on an already-deleted account or duplicate irreversible cleanup.

## Current Behavior

- `db/constraints.py` declares `invoice.account_id ON DELETE RESTRICT`.
- `policy/retention.py` sets finance retention to 30 days.
- `workers/cleanup.py` consumes `account-cleanup` events using `account_id`.

## Decision Needed

How should immediate deletion handle invoices still inside the 30-day retention window?

1. **Immediately delete/anonymize the account while retaining detached finance records for 30 days.**  
   Satisfies immediate account removal and retention, but requires changing the invoice relationship and making cleanup idempotent.

2. **Immediately delete the account and invoices under an explicit retention-policy exception.**  
   Simplest hard delete, but overrides the configured retention policy and may remove required audit data.

3. **Keep finance-linked accounts pending for 30 days, then hard-delete through cleanup.**  
   Preserves retention but does not provide literal immediate account deletion.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `account.delete_flow` has a defined successful result when invoices reference the account.
- The behavior of finance records younger than 30 days is explicit and tested.
- Cleanup safely handles missing accounts, duplicate events, and post-deletion delivery—or the obsolete event path is removed.

## Planning Handoff

Implementation is blocked until one of the three invoice-retention behaviors is selected. No source files were modified.
