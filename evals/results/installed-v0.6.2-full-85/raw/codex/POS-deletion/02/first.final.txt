## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: account.delete_flow would physically remove an account immediately.
- Possible issue: The database rejects deletion for accounts that still have invoice rows, or invoice history must be changed first.
- Affected feature or user: Accounts with invoices and the integrity of invoice.account_id.
- Trigger: Immediate hard deletion of an account referenced by invoice.account_id.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Choose whether to defer physical deletion, anonymize or detach retained invoices, or intentionally remove invoice history and the retention rule.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Immediate account deletion changes the lifetime of account-linked finance data.
- Possible issue: The implementation can violate the configured 30-day finance-retention policy or retain personally identifying account linkage after users believe deletion is complete.
- Affected feature or user: Invoice records, retention enforcement, and account-deletion guarantees.
- Trigger: Deleting an account before FINANCE_RETENTION_DAYS has elapsed.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Define whether immediate deletion means access revocation, identity anonymization, or full physical purge, and align the retention policy and schema with that choice.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-003

- Changed feature: account.delete_flow would complete account deletion immediately while cleanup remains event-driven.
- Possible issue: The worker may receive an ID for an already-deleted account, race the transaction, retry destructive work, or lose cleanup work if event publication is not atomic.
- Affected feature or user: The account-cleanup worker, queue retries, and dependent account data.
- Trigger: Publishing or consuming account-cleanup around an immediate physical delete.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Specify transaction/outbox ordering and make the cleanup handler idempotent and safe when the account no longer exists.
- Status: blocked

## Decision Needed

What must “delete immediately” mean when the account has retained invoices?

1. Immediately disable and anonymize the account, retain invoices for 30 days, then physically delete.
   - Preserves retention and referential integrity, but physical deletion is delayed.

2. Detach or replace `invoice.account_id` with a non-identifying retention reference, then hard-delete the account immediately.
   - Allows immediate account removal while retaining finance records, but requires schema/data migration and an anonymization standard.

3. Cascade-delete invoices and the account immediately, and remove or waive the 30-day retention rule.
   - Provides a full immediate purge, but destroys finance history and changes an explicit retention policy.

## Acceptance and Regression Criteria

- For an account with invoices, `account.delete_flow` completes according to the selected model without foreign-key errors or dangling references.
- Finance-data behavior is tested before and after the 30-day retention boundary.
- Cleanup publication and consumption are idempotent, retry-safe, and tolerate an already-deleted or anonymized account.
