## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: Immediate account deletion through account.delete_flow
- Possible issue: The delete fails for accounts referenced by invoices, or succeeds only after destructive invoice changes.
- Affected feature or user: Accounts with invoices, callers of the deletion flow, and invoice records.
- Trigger: A deletion request targets an account whose id is present in invoice.account_id.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Select an invoice-linked deletion policy and enforce it transactionally before implementing the flow.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Immediate deletion of invoice-linked accounts
- Possible issue: Financial records or their required account linkage are removed before the 30-day retention period ends.
- Affected feature or user: Finance records, retention controls, audits, and account owners with invoice history.
- Trigger: The deletion flow purges or cascades invoice-linked data before its retention deadline.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Retain the minimum finance-linked record for 30 days, or obtain an explicit policy change before permitting an earlier purge.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-003

- Changed feature: Account cleanup orchestration
- Possible issue: Immediate deletion can leave stale cleanup events, duplicate destructive work, or bypass the existing worker contract.
- Affected feature or user: The account-cleanup queue, its worker, retries, and operational monitoring.
- Trigger: account.delete_flow deletes data before an account-cleanup event is consumed or without defining event behavior.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Define emission, idempotency, and missing-account handling for cleanup events as part of the chosen deletion model.
- Status: blocked

### Impact IMP-004

- Changed feature: account.delete_flow implementation in db/constraints.py
- Possible issue: A local edit cannot provide a reliable deletion flow without a defined database API, transaction boundary, and event publisher.
- Affected feature or user: Deletion callers, persistence behavior, and cleanup processing.
- Trigger: Implementation is attempted from the current constant-only module without selecting the lifecycle semantics and integration contract.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Specify the flow contract and verify or add its database and event dependencies before coding.
- Status: blocked

## Decision Needed

- Question: For an account referenced by invoices, what must “delete immediately” mean?

### Option 1

- Option: Immediately deactivate and anonymize non-financial account data, retain the minimum invoice-linked record for 30 days, then publish/consume account-cleanup to hard-delete it.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004
- Trade-off: Preserves retention and gives an immediate user-facing deletion outcome, but physical account deletion is delayed.

### Option 2

- Option: Hard-delete immediately only when no invoices reference the account; otherwise reject or schedule deletion after the 30-day retention period.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004
- Trade-off: Keeps the current constraint and retention policy, but deletion timing differs by invoice history.

### Option 3

- Option: Hard-delete every account immediately and also purge or detach its invoices, with an approved change to the 30-day finance-retention policy and database constraint.
- Affected impacts: IMP-001, IMP-002, IMP-003, IMP-004
- Trade-off: Meets literal immediate physical deletion but is destructive and requires explicit finance-policy and schema authorization.
