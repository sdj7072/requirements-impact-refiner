# Requirements Impact Report

## Report State

- ID: RPT-001
- Revision: 1
- Phase: pre-decision

## Change Impact Summary

### IMP-001 — Dependent invoices

- Severity: critical
- Status: blocked
- Immediate deletion will fail for accounts with invoices because `invoice.account_id` uses `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.
- Loosening the constraint without another strategy could orphan or prematurely destroy retained financial records.

### IMP-002 — Retention policy

- Severity: critical
- Status: blocked
- Immediate deletion conflicts with the 30-day finance-retention rule.
- The repository does not define whether deletion removes only account identity or also retained financial records.

### IMP-003 — Cleanup worker

- Severity: high
- Status: refining
- The cleanup event may run after the account has disappeared.
- Ordering, retries, duplicate delivery, and missing-account behavior are undefined.

## Refined Requirement

Make `account.delete_flow` delete an account immediately while explicitly resolving:

- The invoice foreign-key restriction.
- The 30-day finance-retention obligation.
- The `account-cleanup` event contract.

## Decision Needed

For accounts with invoices inside the 30-day retention window, what should “delete immediately” mean?

1. **Delete identity immediately; retain anonymized invoices for 30 days.**  
   Meets immediate identity deletion and retention, but requires a detached/anonymized invoice reference and idempotent cleanup.

2. **Mark pending deletion; physically delete after 30 days.**  
   Preserves the existing restriction and retention behavior, but physical deletion is not immediate.

3. **Cascade-delete invoices immediately as a policy exception.**  
   Provides immediate physical erasure but violates the current retention rule unless the policy owner approves and updates it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Accounts with and without invoices have a tested deletion result without foreign-key failures or orphaned references.
- Retained invoice data remains valid for the required period without retaining unnecessary account identity.
- The selected 30-day retention interpretation is enforced, including its expiration boundary.
- Immediate identity deletion synchronously removes credentials and non-retained personal data.
- Cleanup succeeds when the account is already absent and when events are delivered more than once.
- Tests define and verify cleanup-event ordering relative to physical deletion.

## Planning Handoff

Implementation is blocked pending selection of option **1, 2, or 3**. No repository files were changed.
