## Change Impact Summary

### Impact IMP-001

- Changed feature: The workspace edit gate admits member in addition to owner and admin.
- Possible issue: Every invited member gains edit capability across all projects governed by this gate, including projects they do not own.
- Affected feature or user: Workspace members and all projects protected by authorization.workspace_edit.
- Trigger: authorize_project_edit is called with role member.
- Severity: critical
- Prevention or check: Make the global scope explicit in tests and limit the code change to the requested role allowlist.
- Status: accepted

### Impact IMP-002

- Changed feature: Successful member edits become part of the existing project edit audit stream.
- Possible issue: Audit consumers may begin seeing role member for a previously privileged-only action.
- Affected feature or user: Audit event consumers and operational monitoring for project edits.
- Trigger: A newly authorized member edit emits project.edit.audited.
- Severity: medium
- Prevention or check: Verify that member remains present in the emitted role field and keep the event name and payload shape unchanged.
- Status: accepted

### Impact IMP-003

- Changed feature: The allowlist is widened without changing existing privileged roles.
- Possible issue: Owner or admin edit access could regress if the tuple is replaced rather than extended.
- Affected feature or user: Workspace owners and admins.
- Trigger: The workspace edit permission constant is modified.
- Severity: high
- Prevention or check: Assert owner, admin, and member are authorized and an unrelated role remains denied.
- Status: accepted
